Rules & Policies

Updated July 15, 2026. Mister Rin.

General

I only draw anthropomorphic characters.

You must provide a reference sheet for your character. If you do not have one, you must give me a detailed written description of their appearance along with a finished visual color palette.

You may also provide photo references and 3D references if available.

Payment

Payment is accepted via PayPal in USD.

Other payment methods may also be discussed if you are, for example, from the CIS or China.

If you are commissioning me together with a friend, please note that I only accept payment through a single invoice, not two separate ones.

Full payment is only required after the sketch is finished and approved. I do not accept payment before providing a sketch and receiving your approval!

Payment plans are available for orders of $200 or more and can be split into two or three payments. You will receive the finished artwork only after the final payment, but WIPs will be provided throughout the process. This is simply to protect me as the artist from potential fraud.

Process

In addition to Trello, after the sketch is approved, you will receive a Google Drive link for your commission. It will contain a separate folder for WIPs, and the source file of the finished commission will also be uploaded there in the highest available quality.

I do this in every case, even if you receive updates and the finished commission through Telegram.

I usually share relatively few WIPs because of my perfectionism, mostly showing the main stages: sketch, lineart, and finished artwork. However, if you would like to receive WIPs more often and in real time because you are interested or worried, just tell me!

I include 2-3 revisions for free. Any additional revisions will cost extra. Corrections involving mistakes in the character, their design, or anything similar are free, even after the artwork is fully finished, but only if it was genuinely my mistake.

Please note that I do not charge extra for complex character designs, such as characters with many spots or stripes, because I simplify those details. I still try to keep the placement of markings and similar features accurate within reason.

Deadline

Since I am more of a creator than a craftsman, I do not work with strict deadlines unless one has been discussed in advance.

A commission usually takes 1-2 months depending on the size of my queue, but if you need a more specific estimate, we can discuss it.

I organize my work based on complexity, my current workload, and my creative capacity, but I always try to create the highest-quality artwork I am capable of making for you.

You may publish it, post it, use it as an avatar, and do anything you want with it for personal use, as long as you do not crop out my signature from the finished artwork. Cropping it for an avatar is fine.

You must also credit me as the artist on any website where you publish my work.

You may not publish any of my content on paid platforms or use it as paid content. Anything is allowed except commercial use.

You may not train any local AI model on my artwork or use it to replicate my style. Dude, it still will not capture emotions as well as I do anyway lol.

During the commission process, you must tell me in advance if you do not want me to identify you or your username as the client. In that case, I will publish the commission anonymously without mentioning you or your username.

You must also tell me in advance if you do not want the commission published at all, or if you do not want a more sensitive part of it published. In that case, the commission will remain completely private and available only to you.

You do not need to worry about this! I do not charge extra for privacy.

Refunds

A refund is possible if I have not started your commission or if I am completely unable to complete it. The most convenient refund method will be discussed privately.

Once work has begun, refunds are generally not available. However, if I am unable to complete the commission, or if no meaningful progress has been made, a refund may still be issued.
