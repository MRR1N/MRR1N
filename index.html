General

I only draw anthropomorphic characters.

You must provide a reference sheet for your character. If you do not have one, you must give me a detailed written description of their appearance along with a finished visual color palette.

You may also provide photo references and 3D references if available.

Payment

Payment is accepted via PayPal in USD.

Other payment methods may also be discussed if you are, for example, from the CIS or China.

If you are commissioning me together with a friend, please note that I only accept payment through a single invoice, not two separate ones.

Full payment is only required after the sketch is finished and approved. I do not accept payment before providing a sketch and receiving your approval.

Payment plans are available for orders of $200 or more and can be split into two or three payments. You will receive the finished artwork only after the final payment, but WIPs will be provided throughout the process.

Process

In addition to Trello, after the sketch is approved, you will receive a Google Drive link for your commission. It will contain a separate folder for WIPs, and the source file of the finished commission will also be uploaded there in the highest available quality.

I do this in every case, even if you receive updates and the finished commission through Telegram.

I usually share relatively few WIPs because of my perfectionism, mostly showing the main stages: sketch, lineart, and finished artwork. However, if you would like to receive WIPs more often and in real time, just tell me.

I include 2–3 revisions for free. Any additional revisions will cost extra. Corrections involving mistakes in the character or their design are free, even after the artwork is finished, if it was genuinely my mistake.

I do not charge extra for complex character designs such as many spots or stripes because I simplify those details while keeping their placement accurate within reason.

Deadline

Since I am more of a creator than a craftsman, I do not work with strict deadlines unless one has been discussed in advance.

A commission usually takes 1–2 months depending on the size of my queue, but if you need a more specific estimate, we can discuss it.

I organize my work based on complexity, current workload and creative capacity, while aiming for the highest quality I can provide.

Rights

Purchasing a commission does not transfer copyright ownership to you.

You may publish it, post it, use it as an avatar, and use it personally as long as you do not remove my signature from the finished artwork. Cropping it for an avatar is fine.

You must credit me as the artist on websites where you publish my work.

You may not publish my content on paid platforms or use it as paid content unless commercial use has been agreed separately.

You may not train a local AI model on my artwork or use it to replicate my style.

I may use your commission for promotional purposes unless we have agreed otherwise. You can request anonymous publication or complete privacy in advance, and I do not charge extra for privacy.

Refunds

A refund is possible if I have not started your commission or if I am completely unable to complete it. The most convenient refund method will be discussed privately.

Once work has begun, refunds are generally unavailable. If I am unable to complete the commission or no meaningful progress has been made, a refund may still be issued.

What will I not draw?

Diapers, muscles or bodybuilders, hyper genitalia, fat characters or characters with extreme body proportions, feral characters, pregnancy, ponies, lactation, any kind of transformation, or castration.

Other than that, I have no taboos.

I will be happy to help you come up with an idea or story if you are having trouble with it.

I will also be happy to receive creative freedom, but first I will need to learn more about your character and their backstory.
